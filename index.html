Weekly Practice Schedule

Every practice for every group, at both of our pools, on one calendar that is always current. Each block names its pool, so you never have to work out which table you are reading.

✓

Get updates for your swimmer’s group

When your group’s practice moves, is cut short, or the pool closes, you get one email about that group — not every message for the whole club.

  1. In the calendar below, press Subscribe at the right end of the filter row.
  2. Pick your swimmer’s group, enter your email and press Send updates. Confirm from the email you receive.
  3. Optional: add the group to Apple, Google or Outlook Calendar from the same panel.

On the calendar there is no separate exceptions list: a changed day simply is the changed day, in the week it happens, with its pool on it. Past exceptions drop out on their own, and the families following a group hear about a change the moment it is made — no WhatsApp thread to scroll back through.

What this replaces

  • Two tables that never change, plus a list that always does. The weekly pattern and its exceptions are one calendar here, so nobody has to merge them in their head.
  • Exceptions that outlive their date. The list above keeps growing at the bottom while its top slips into the past. The calendar only shows what is still ahead.
  • “Scroll left on mobile app.” On a phone the calendar reads as a list, one practice per line.
  • Two pools, one page. La Jolla and Coronado groups sit side by side, and every block names its pool — Triton, Marcikic or the Competition Pool.
  • Updates in WhatsApp. A parent subscribes to just their swimmer’s group and gets an email when that group’s practice moves. Club news can stay in WhatsApp.
  • Pool closures can arrive with the pool’s own schedule. UC San Diego’s and the City of Coronado’s schedules are in Pool Relay too, so a closed pool can show on the practice it affects instead of waiting for someone to retype the table.
